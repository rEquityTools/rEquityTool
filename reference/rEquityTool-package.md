# rEquityTool: Wealth Scores and Wealth Quintiles from 'EquityTool' Questionnaires

Reads the country workbooks published by the 'EquityTool'
(<https://equitytool.org>), a simplified asset-based measure of relative
household wealth derived from Demographic and Health Survey and Multiple
Indicator Cluster Survey wealth indices, and applies them to survey
data. Given a data frame of respondent answers, the package computes the
'EquityTool' wealth score and assigns respondents to wealth quintiles
using the published national, urban or rural cut-points, or using the
sample's own weighted score distribution when relative position within
the surveyed population is the quantity of interest. Response coding is
validated before scoring, and score tables are cross-checked against the
two independent representations of them in each workbook.
